# Venue

**The UMI Arena**, a workshop at CoRL 2026 (Austin, TX). Workshop day: Nov 12, 2026.
Organised by AIRoA. Site: <https://umi-arena.airoa.io/> (checked 2026-10-16).

This paper (`paper-headcam/`) is written for **Track 3 (Contributed Papers)**.
The device-track paper in `../paper/` was for Track 2.

## Track 3: Contributed Papers (target)

- **2–4 page extended abstract, excluding acknowledgements and references**.
  The workshop intro also calls them "4-page, non-archival".
- Template: **CoRL main-conference format** (`corl_2026.sty`, `[preprint]`).
- Review: **single-blind** (authors shown), judged by the program committee.
- Submission: **OpenReview**.
- Dates: submission **Oct 16, 2026** (no time zone given; a third-party
  tracker lists Oct 17 05:59 UTC; confirm on OpenReview), notification
  **Oct 27, 2026**.
- Award: Best Paper, $1,000 plus a YUBI set.
- Workshop topics this draft targets (from the comment in `main.tex`):
  in-the-wild tracking beyond SLAM; the egocentric viewpoint gap between
  collection and deployment; data scaling and curation.

## Track 2: Device Exhibition (for reference)

- 2–4 page short paper plus a 1–3 minute video showing rollouts of policies
  trained on the device's data. Whether references count is not stated.
- Judged on the attendee-vote criteria: ergonomics, dexterity, strength,
  reproducibility, cost, etc.
- Applications closed Oct 9; exhibitors notified Oct 16. At least one team
  member in person on Nov 12 with the device. Award: Best Device, $2,500.
