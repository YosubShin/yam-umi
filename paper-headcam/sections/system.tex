\section{System}
\label{sec:system}

\paragraph{Collection.}
The glove is an unpowered printed replica of the YAM gripper \citep{i2rtyam} from \citet{yamumi2026}, stock fingers on a rack-and-pinion, with an 11-face \SI{15}{mm} ArUco dodecahedron \citep{garrido2014aruco,wu2017dodecapen} on the back of the hand. Fingertip tags facing a wrist camera \citep{chi2024umi} are edge-on from the head, so each jaw carries a printed wedge, the \emph{tail}, with \SI{12}{mm} AprilTags on five faces beside a fixed tag on the gripper back: one face is always visible, and the tail-to-back distance along the jaw axis gives the width. Parts cost \$22. The collector wears an Arducam B0587 4K camera (\$122) on a \$10 GoPro head strap (\SI{103}{g} worn), recording at \SI{15}{Hz} with a short exposure, aimed once per session so the frame centre coincides with the collector's own (App.~\ref{app:details}). Four \SI{60}{mm} AprilTag tiles define the workspace frame, placed where the gaze lands during grasping so that two stay in view, with no other tag in the scene. Continuous recording with keypress episode boundaries gives 2.0--3.1 pick-and-place demonstrations per minute (median \SI{6}{}--\SI{8}{s}) against 1.6 by teleoperation on the same arm (median \SI{14}{s}).

\paragraph{Labels from the head camera.}
\label{sec:labels}
Each frame yields (i) the camera pose from the tiles, (ii) the gripper pose in the camera frame from PnP over the visible ball faces, and (iii) the jaw width from the tails by a per-unit linear map (\SI{0.9}{}--\SI{3.8}{mm} holdout RMS on calipered spacers). Composing (i) and (ii) gives the tool pose in the tile frame; a one-time link to the robot base, fitted with the ball mounted on the YAM (App.~\ref{app:details}), turns it into joint-space actions comparable to teleoperation. Two mechanisms keep the camera localised under head motion: the tile layout is refit from the multi-tile frames of the footage itself (four-tile reprojection \SI{3.4}{px} to \SI{0.5}{px} RMS), and frames with a single tile are chained to the previous accepted pose under velocity bounds rather than discarded. Dropouts over \SI{0.3}{s} split an episode and segments under \SI{1}{s} are dropped: \SI{0.7}{}--\SI{7.4}{\percent} of frames are cut per session, 200 segments from 198 demonstrations. A fixed external camera on the same demonstrations loses \SI{8}{}--\SI{22}{\percent} to body occlusion and fragments them into 259 segments.

\paragraph{The robot's head.}
A six-joint SO-101 follower arm \citep{so101} (six Feetech STS3215 servos, \$122 in parts) stands beside the YAM and carries the same camera on a GoPro-pattern mount in place of its gripper (Fig.~\ref{fig:teaser}c). Its kinematics are fitted once from 60 tile-verified poses (\SI{7}{mm} / \SI{0.7}{\degree} residual). At each session start it parks at the collector's median head pose and registers against the tiles; thereafter it tracks the gripper from the YAM's joint encoders alone, transparent to the policy: a fixed-gain controller holds the gripper's bearing and a ridge-regression residual fitted on about 100 glove episodes reproduces head motion relative to the hand (\SI{46}{mm} / \SI{5.4}{\degree} median head-pose error on held-out episodes against \SI{87}{mm} / \SI{7.0}{\degree} for a static camera; App.~\ref{app:details}). The camera follows the gripper with \SI{0.4}{s} lag and \SI{16}{mm} median tile-referenced error, and frames it where the collector did (normalised gripper centroid 0.37/0.62 against 0.38/0.60).

\paragraph{Datasets and training.}
Teleoperation under the positioner gave 178 episodes (38.6k frames; 6 held out for validation); four glove sessions gave 198 demonstrations, 200 segments (50.0k frames; 6 held out). All policies are ACT \citep{zhao2023act} in LeRobot \citep{lerobot2024}, 50k steps, joint-space actions, best checkpoint by validation loss on the held-out teleoperated episodes. Following \citet{yamumi2026}, glove timelines are resampled to teleoperation pace and widths mapped into the gripper command space, with the closed state anchored to the teleoperators' minimum commanded squeeze (0.22 of stroke rather than the 0.25 median): the head-view policies released no block during the carry in 120 rollouts.
